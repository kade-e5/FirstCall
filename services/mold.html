Complete mold removal is a complex and potentially hazardous job that should always be handled by trained professionals. Will my insurance policy cover mold damage?

Reach out to your insurance broker to confirm whether your policy covers mold remediation and, if so, the specific limits and coverage details. Policies vary widely in terms of what types of mold damage are included (or excluded) and the maximum dollar amount your insurer will pay, so getting clarity directly from your broker ensures you understand your exact benefits.

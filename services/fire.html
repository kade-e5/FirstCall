Particulate Soot Decontamination

Particulate Soot Decontamination targets the tiny, harmful soot particles and toxic residues that linger after a fire or puff back. These microscopic, often oily contaminants penetrate deep into surfaces, HVAC systems, and porous materials—posing serious health risks through poor air quality and long-term exposure. Professional removal is essential, as they can be invisible, odorless, or difficult to detect.

First Call uses advanced HEPA air scrubbers, specialized vacuums, chemical sponges, and targeted air purification to thoroughly capture and eliminate these particulates from surfaces, ducts, and the air.

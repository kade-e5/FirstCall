Water Damage: What to Do and What NOT to Do

What To Do:

  • If possible, turn off your home’s water supply.
  • Removing excess water: Use a mop to soak up as much standing water as feasible. During cooler conditions, open windows to promote faster evaporation. In warmer conditions, activate the air conditioner provided it's safe and appropriate.
  • Be careful: Exercise extreme caution when moving through the affected area. Be vigilant for slip, trip, and fall hazards, as well as exposed electrical wiring.
  • Elevate upholstered items: Move any removable cushions and upholstered furniture to a dry area, propping them up to allow air circulation and promote thorough drying.
  • Safeguard valuables: Relocate paintings, artwork, books, electronics, and any other precious or sensitive items to a secure, dry location away from the affected area to prevent further damage.
  • Clear the floor: Remove all wet rugs immediately. Pick up any items, furniture, or objects on the floor that could become tripping hazards while the area is wet.
  • Dry wet containers: Open any soaked suitcases, storage bins, or boxes, or similar items immediately. If weather permits, place them in direct sunlight to dry thoroughly; otherwise, set them in a well-ventilated area with lids off and contents removed.
  • Dry wet clothing promptly: Launder or air-dry all soaked clothing items right away to prevent mildew. For fur and leather goods, hang them in a well-ventilated area at normal room temperature to dry naturally, avoiding direct heat sources.
  • Protect furniture on wet carpet: Place aluminum foil, plastic wrap, or similar barriers under the legs of furniture to prevent rust stains or wood damage. Gently open drawers and cabinet doors (without forcing them) to allow air circulation and dry the interiors thoroughly.
  • Call First Call Immediately: Contact First Call of Durango. Our certified technicians are experts in Durango water damage repair.

What Not To Do:

  • AVOID TOUCHING PLUGS OR OUTLETS: Do not plug in or unplug any electrical appliances, and avoid touching anything that is already plugged in.
  • AVOID USING ELECTRICAL DEVICES OR APPLIANCES: Do not touch or operate any electrical items, including televisions, computers, radios, fans, or anything plugged into an outlet—especially if you are standing on a wet floor, no matter what the surface material is.
  • AVOID USING A REGULAR VACUUM: Do not use a standard household vacuum cleaner. Only use a wet/dry vacuum designed specifically for extracting water.
  • AVOID EXCESSIVE HEAT: Keep damp areas as cool as possible, since warm and moist conditions promote mold growth.
